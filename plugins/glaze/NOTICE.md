## Test harness

`reference/` builds a Rust program that depends, unmodified, on the GPUI crates above to render reference images and dump reference data. It contains one adapted copy of GPUI code: `reference/src/unhinted_text.rs` copies `render_glyph_image` from gpui-pre-wgpu (Zed Industries, Apache-2.0) with hinting disabled, to rasterize glyphs like GPUI does on macOS. The files it produces (`test/golden/gpui/*`, `tests/unit/data/gpui-*.json`) are outputs of GPUI (Copyright Zed Industries, Inc., Apache-2.0), and `tests/unit/data/gpui-kit-themes-reference.json` is output of gpui-kit (Apache-2.0) and rust-i18n (MIT); they are used as test oracles.

`test/golden/compare.js` and the structure of `test/golden/run-golden.sh` and `test/golden/src/GoldenScene.hx` come from the golden-image harness of the `star` Ceramic plugin.
